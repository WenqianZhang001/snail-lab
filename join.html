Build sensing systems with us

The lab is new and growing. If you want to work on problems that span signal processing, hardware, learning, and real robots — and you want to see your system run outside a simulator — we would like to hear from you.

Prospective PhD students

Apply directly to the Yale graduate program in Electrical Engineering or Computer Science and name the SNAIL Lab and Prof. Boroushaki in your statement of purpose.

Current Yale students

Contact the PI directly: email tara.boroushaki@yale.edu with your CV and transcript.

Postdoctoral researchers

We consider postdoctoral applications on a rolling basis. Send your CV, two representative papers, and a short research statement describing what you would want to build here.
